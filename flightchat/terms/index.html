Blocking and reporting

  • Block: in a one-on-one chat, tap ⋯ then Block or Report, or long-press someone's message and tap the hand. You stop seeing their messages, and they can't message you. You can unblock people in Settings.
  • Report: choose Report and Block. Your email app opens with a report to biomass-65-jumbo@icloud.com. If you're offline, it's sent once your phone is back online.

We'll reply to every report within 24 hours of receiving it. Because FlightChat has no servers or accounts, we can't read your chats, delete messages from other people's phones, or ban anyone. We rely on the report you send, and when content may be illegal or someone may be in danger, we may pass the report to the authorities.

If you feel unsafe on board, tell a crew member straight away.
