What it does

Where Am I? shows your iPhone's live GPS readings: latitude and longitude, altitude above sea level, ground speed and heading, with a signal-strength antenna. It also keeps trip statistics, draws your track on a map, exports it as a GPX file, and can show your speed and altitude on the Lock Screen.

Getting a good signal

  • GPS works without internet or cell service, including in airplane mode.
  • Indoors, or in a plane, sit by a window. Metal and concrete block most of the signal.
  • The first fix can take a few minutes without internet, because your iPhone can't download its usual satellite assistance data.
  • The antenna shows how good the fix is. If it greys out, the app shows your last reading and its time until the signal returns.

Saving battery

The screen uses most of the power. In the app's Settings, turn off Keep screen awake to let your iPhone lock, and turn off High-precision GPS for a small saving. Show on Lock Screen keeps GPS running in the background, so turn it off when you don't need it.
