The short version

Where Am I? shows your iPhone's GPS readings: your position, altitude, ground speed, heading and signal strength. It can also record a trip you choose to record. Your location is used on your phone and is never sent to us: we don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and there's no analytics or advertising. The app uses the internet only to load Apple Maps when you view a saved trip, to show the name of the street and town you're in on CarPlay, and, if iCloud Drive is on, to keep your saved trips in your own iCloud account.

Your location

  • The app reads your location only while it's open on screen, on your car's CarPlay screen, or while you're recording a trip.
  • While a trip is recording, the app keeps reading your location in the background until you tap Stop. iOS shows a blue location indicator while this happens.
  • If you turn on Show on Lock Screen in the app's Settings, it keeps reading your location while it's in the background, to keep the Live Activity up to date. iOS shows a blue location indicator while this happens. Turn the setting off, or close the app, to stop it.
  • Your location is processed on your iPhone and is never sent to us. The one exception to keeping it on the phone is the place name on CarPlay, below.

What's stored on your phone

Where Am I? stores your settings, such as units and coordinate format, on your iPhone. It keeps a history of where you've been only when you record a trip: the trip's route (positions, altitude, speed and times) and its totals are saved until you tap Reset or delete the saved trip. If iCloud Drive is on, saved trips are stored in your iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone; otherwise they're kept on your iPhone. A trip still being recorded is always kept on your iPhone. Your iCloud data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we can't see it. Readings you don't record are shown and then discarded. Nothing the app stores is sent to us or to any third party.

Maps

When you open a saved trip while your phone is online, the app shows it on Apple Maps. To draw the map, your iPhone downloads map images of the trip's area from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy for Maps. Your route is drawn on your phone and isn't sent to Apple or to us. Offline, the map isn't shown.

Place names on CarPlay

On your car's CarPlay screen, the app shows the name of the street and town you're in. To get it, your iPhone sends your current position to Apple's geocoding service, at most once a minute while CarPlay is showing the app, under Apple's privacy policy for Maps. Nothing is sent when the app isn't on the CarPlay screen, and nothing is sent to us.

Snapshots

When you tap Save, the app creates an image and a text summary of your current readings on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. They go only where you choose to save or send them. Remember that they contain your location.

On the Lock Screen

With Show on Lock Screen on, your speed, altitude and coordinates appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, where anyone who can see your phone can read them.

What we never collect

No analytics or advertising code runs in Where Am I?. The app doesn't send your location, contacts, phone number, email address, advertising identifier or device information anywhere, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use these only to fix bugs. You can turn this off under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • To delete a saved trip, swipe it left in the Trips list, or open it and choose Delete. Reset deletes the trip being recorded.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Trips saved to iCloud stay there, so they come back if you reinstall the app. To delete them, delete the trips in the app, or go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Where Am I.
  • Snapshots you saved or sent elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Children

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
