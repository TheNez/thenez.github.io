The short version

Where Am I? shows your iPhone's GPS readings: your position, altitude, ground speed, heading and signal strength. Your location is used on your phone to show those readings and is never sent to us. We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and there's no analytics or advertising in the app.

Your location

  • The app reads your location only while it's open on screen.
  • If you turn on Show on Lock Screen in the app's Settings, it keeps reading your location while it's in the background, to keep the Live Activity up to date. iOS shows a blue location indicator while this happens. Turn the setting off, or close the app, to stop it.
  • Your location is processed on your iPhone and is never sent to us or to any third party by the app.

What's stored on your phone

Where Am I? stores the following only on your iPhone, inside the app:

  • Your settings, such as units and coordinate format
  • Your current trip: distance, duration, maximum speed and altitude, and the recorded track (positions, altitudes, speeds and times), until you tap Reset

None of this is sent to us or to any third party. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS includes the app's storage in the backup. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

Maps

The map uses Apple Maps. To draw it, your iPhone downloads map images for the area on screen from Apple when you have an internet connection. Apple handles those requests under the Apple Maps privacy policy. We never see them. Without an internet connection, such as on a plane, the map is blank but your position and track still show.

Snapshots and GPX exports

When you tap Save or Export GPX, the app creates an image, a text summary or a GPX track file on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to save or send it. Remember that these files contain your location.

On the Lock Screen

With Show on Lock Screen on, your speed, altitude and coordinates appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, where anyone who can see your phone can read them.

What we never collect

No analytics or advertising code runs in Where Am I?. The app doesn't send your location, contacts, phone number, email address, advertising identifier or device information anywhere, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use these only to fix bugs. You can turn this off under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • To erase your trip and recorded track, tap Reset on the Trip card.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Snapshots and GPX files you saved or sent elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Children

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Where Am I? handles your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Any change that affects your data will also be described in the app's release notes.
